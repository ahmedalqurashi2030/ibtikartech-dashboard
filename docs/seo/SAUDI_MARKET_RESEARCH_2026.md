# Ibtikar Tech — Saudi Market Keyword Research 2026

Status: Phase 5 evidence report
Market: Saudi Arabia first, GCC second
Research date: 2026-09-18
Canonical site: https://ibtikartech.co

## Research scope

This report records the market evidence behind the Phase 5 keyword and intent decisions. It studies current Arabic/Saudi search-result patterns, competitor landing pages, pricing/comparison content, and official Salla/Zid material.

It does not invent search volume, KD, CPC, exact Google positions, or conversion rates. Direct Google SERP pages were not accessible through the research interface, so current web-search samples and destination pages are used for qualitative intent/page-type analysis.

## Core finding

Saudi search demand around Ibtikar Tech services is not one funnel. It repeatedly splits into four layers:

1. Learning/problem discovery — كيف / ما هو / خطوات / مشاكل.
2. Commercial investigation — شركة / أفضل / أسعار / تكلفة / مقارنة.
3. Transactional execution — تصميم / إنشاء / تطوير / تخصيص / تحسين / ربط.
4. Product/navigation — known product or brand terms such as ثيم ثراء.

This means a service page, a cost guide, a comparison guide, and a how-to article should not all target the same primary task.

Market implication:
تصميم متجر سلة is an umbrella commercial phrase, not a clean specialist query. It should belong to the ecommerce hub, while specialist pages own narrower modifiers.
